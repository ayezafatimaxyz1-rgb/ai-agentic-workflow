# Buyer research: premium leather jackets

Gathered from buyer reviews, forums and trade guides. Collected for Phase 3 of the 10k-websites build. The point of this file is the buyers' own words, which the site's copy is written in.

## The objections that stop the sale

Ranked by how often they came up.

1. **"It is going to be fake."** The phrase "genuine leather" is widely known to be a low grade, not a promise. Buyers describe getting jackets that "looked nothing like the photo," PU that "didn't smell like leather," a "uniform textured pattern" that gives away machine stamping, and the water test: real hide absorbs, fake beads.
2. **"It will not fit and I will be stuck with it."** Sleeves "quite large in circumference," petite sizes "too tight," and the fear behind it: restocking fees, overseas return shipping, sellers who "dismissed complaints and refused returns."
3. **"It will crack."** Bonded and cheap leather "looks great on the rack" then cracks and flakes within two to five years.
4. **"It is too stiff."** Buyers expect a break in period and fear the jacket that never softens. Stiffness that does not resolve reads as the tell of artificial leather.
5. **"That is a lot of money."** A well made moto or bomber lands in the $300 to $800 range and up. The price itself is not the objection. Unjustified price is.

## What they actually want

- **Something that gets better, not worse.** The single strongest desire in the premium end. Full grain "improves visually with use rather than degrading." Patina is the word buyers use for it, and they want it by name.
- **Decades, not seasons.** Premium hides quoted at 10 to 25 years with care. Cheap ones at 2 to 5.
- **To be able to tell.** Buyers want to know how to verify what they bought: the grain, the smell, the absorption, the weight.
- **Drape.** The lambskin buyer wants smooth and supple that stays looking new. The full grain buyer wants character. These are two different customers and the site should be honest about which one it serves.

## What this means for the page

- Answer objection 1 visually and in words, early. Name the hide by type.
- Objection 2 is solved by a real fit and returns answer, in the FAQ, in plain words.
- Objections 3 and 4 are answered by the same content: what the leather does over years.
- Do not apologise for the price. Justify it with the lifespan and the hide, which is what the buyer is already looking for.
